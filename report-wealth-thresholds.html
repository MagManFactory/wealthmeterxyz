The hidden problem

A percentile tells you where you stand. It does not tell you what doors open.

Gross net worth, investable assets, income, collateral, and geography are routinely collapsed into one flattering number. The report separates them, then maps the practical discontinuities created by liquidity, accreditation, provider minimums, and service economics.

Inside the report

A decision map, not another rich list.

  • Four access bands: $10K-$100K, $1M-$5M, $30M+, and $1B+, with the limits of each classification made explicit.
  • What opens at each threshold: liquidity, lending, private-market access, advisory economics, custody, governance, and jurisdictional options.
  • Three pathway scenarios: a professional accumulator, an entrepreneurial exit, and an inheritance recipient.
  • Compounding comparison: how initial capital, contribution rate, and time horizon alter the path.
  • Legal and structural vehicles: an at-a-glance comparison of the entities and coordination needs that emerge by tier.
  • Onboarding, fees, and due diligence: the friction most threshold charts omit.
