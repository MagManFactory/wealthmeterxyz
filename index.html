How WealthMeter Works

WealthMeter converts your entered net worth to USD, maps that value onto a calibrated global wealth distribution curve, then calculates your estimated global rank and percentile using the selected adult population baseline.

Input

Your total net worth and currency. Optional baseline selection lets you compare covered-market versus global-adult denominator context.

Model

Piecewise log interpolation for broad distribution and Pareto tail treatment for high-net-worth ranges to avoid linear distortion at the top.

Outputs

Global rank, percentile, and country-relative perspective snapshots for contextual benchmarking.

Why can rank change when baseline changes?

The denominator changes. UBS covered-market and UN WPP global-adult baselines represent different population scopes.

Is this investment advice?

No. Outputs are educational planning estimates and not financial, legal, or tax advice.
