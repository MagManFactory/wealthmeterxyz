How WealthMeter Works

WealthMeter converts your entered net worth to USD using dated ECB reference rates, then maps it to the latest UBS covered-market distribution and current upper-tail counts. The output is an estimate across the 56 markets in the UBS sample.

Input

Your individual net worth and currency. Use assets less debts, include funded private retirement assets, and divide jointly owned household wealth consistently between adults.

Model

Piecewise log interpolation between published wealth-band and upper-tail population anchors. No single tail exponent is extended across every high-wealth tier.

Outputs

Covered-market rank, conventional percentile, top-share, and population-scale comparisons. Below USD 10,000, the output is a published range.

Why is this not a whole-world rank?

UBS models 56 markets representing more than 92% of world wealth. WealthMeter does not assume that uncovered markets have the same distribution. For a U.S. household benchmark, use the U.S. Household Wealth Calculator.

Is this investment advice?

No. Outputs are educational planning estimates and not financial, legal, or tax advice.
