Trust and interpretation

Definitions before conclusions.

How to read these comparisons

Years can differ because each provider publishes on its own schedule. Comparisons always display the observation year beside each value. Derived findings combine only compatible measures and retain the contributing sources.

What was excluded

A former consumption-to-GDP percentage was removed because it divided a constant-dollar consumption series by a current-international-dollar GDP series. The units were not compatible. OECD EO107 observations are retained only in the historical pandemic archive.
