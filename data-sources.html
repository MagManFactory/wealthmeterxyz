Data Sources.

WealthMeter anchors each calculator to current primary research and identifies which sources drive calculations versus contextual analysis.

Last updated: Sep 17, 2026

Primary Global Distribution UBS Global Wealth Report 2026
Year-end 2025 wealth pyramid, covered-market adult denominator, millionaire tiers, and billionaire counts.
Upper-Tail Cross-Check Knight Frank Wealth Sizing Model 2026
Published count of people with net worth above USD 30 million.
Currency Conversion European Central Bank Reference Rates
Calculator snapshot dated September 15, 2026.
U.S. Household Wealth U.S. Census Bureau 2024 Wealth and Asset Ownership Tables
2025 SIPP household wealth bands for calendar year 2024, released July 15, 2026. Download the official detailed workbook. Table 4 provides the calculator's nine published bands and comparison-group shares.
U.S. Survey Methodology Survey of Income and Program Participation Methodology
Definitions, sampling, imputation, weighting, and error treatment for the Census source series.
U.S. Household Upper Tail Federal Reserve 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances
The public summary extract, including all five implicates and the WGT analysis weight, divides the Census table's open USD 500,000-or-more category. The current public SCF release remains 2022. Its family definition is not identical to the Census household definition, so the result is a calibrated model rather than a published rank.
Dollar-Year Adjustment BLS CPI-U Annual Averages
SCF 2022 net-worth values are converted to 2024 dollars using 313.689 ÷ 292.655.
Contextual Inequality Research World Inequality Database
Used for editorial context, not as an operational anchor in the current homepage calculator.
Global Wealth Data Lab Versioned source and definition register
The interactive Data Lab records the source, definition, caveat, observation year, and record identifier for World Bank, WID, Census IDB, and separately labeled historical OECD observations.
Quality-Adjusted Purchasing Power World Bank World Development Indicators
Private-consumption PPP and official exchange rates supply national price levels. Life expectancy, electricity access, internet use, safely managed sanitation, transport infrastructure, and intentional homicide supply the quality constraints. The interface exposes observation years and underlying source organizations; missing values are not imputed.

Source register reviewed September 17, 2026. The homepage uses UBS 2026 covered-market counts and a Knight Frank 2026 upper-tail anchor. The U.S. calculator uses published Census household bands below USD 500,000 and a Census-calibrated Federal Reserve SCF curve above that threshold. Wealth Explorer country medians are also drawn from UBS 2026 but answer a different question from the rank calculator.
