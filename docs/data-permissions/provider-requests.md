# Country Systems Atlas provider permission requests

Prepared for review on 2026-09-16. These messages are drafts only. Michelle Martin sends them after clearance. Preserve each tracking ID in the subject line so replies remain auditable.

## ITU

**To:** indicators@itu.int  
**Subject:** [CS-ITU-20260916] Permission request: selected ITU indicators in free country-comparison atlas

Dear ITU ICT Data and Analytics team,

I am writing on behalf of WealthMeter.xyz and LifeMeter.xyz to request permission to use selected country-level indicators from the ITU DataHub in a free, publicly accessible Country Systems Atlas.

The proposed atlas would allow visitors to compare countries using measures such as population coverage by mobile-network technology, internet adoption, fixed-broadband subscriptions, and ICT service affordability. The atlas would display selected observations and derived visualizations rather than provide a bulk download or reproduce the complete ITU database.

The intended transformations include country comparisons, changes over time, per-capita or percentage displays where methodologically appropriate, ranks or percentiles within a clearly identified comparison group, and scatterplots combining an ITU measure with separately licensed indicators. Every display would identify ITU, the ITU DataHub, the indicator, observation year, and access date. We would not use the ITU logo, imply endorsement, or sell or sublicense the underlying ITU data.

The atlas itself would be available without charge. WealthMeter.xyz and LifeMeter.xyz are mixed-purpose editorial websites that also publish and sell reports and collect newsletter subscriptions. Links to related editorial material or reports may appear elsewhere on the sites. We are therefore seeking express written permission rather than assuming that the non-commercial licence applies.

Could ITU confirm whether this proposed use is permitted and identify any required attribution, share-alike, technical-access, or other conditions? If a separate licence or agreement is required, please direct us to the appropriate process.

Thank you,

Michelle Martin  
WealthMeter.xyz and LifeMeter.xyz

We would not use the WHO name or emblem as branding, imply endorsement, sell or sublicense the underlying WHO data, or use individual-level information.

Related editorial or report links may appear elsewhere on the sites. Because WHO's published data terms refer to public-health purposes, commercial-enterprise promotion, and authorization for modifications beyond minimal restyling, we are requesting express written approval for this context and for the described analytical transformations.

Could WHO confirm whether this proposed use is permitted and identify the required attribution, disclaimer, transformation, and technical-access conditions? If this request must proceed through the formal permissions process, please route us to the appropriate team or form.
