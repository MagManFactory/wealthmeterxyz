Affiliate partnerships

Useful financial products, introduced at the relevant decision point.

WealthMeter is considering affiliate inquiries from products and services that improve financial measurement, planning, or execution. Current review categories include tax and budgeting tools, portfolio-analysis software, retirement-planning services, cash-management and savings products, carefully reviewed insurance comparison services, and financial education or data subscriptions. Compensation cannot buy a ranking, recommendation, or favorable result.

What may be consideredContextual links, clearly labeled recommendation modules, or a limited hybrid underwriting-and-performance arrangement.
What to includeProgram terms, commission model, attribution window, tracking method, privacy policy, eligible geographies, and every proposed financial or performance claim.
What remains independentCalculator outputs, rankings, editorial conclusions, report recommendations, methodology, and correction decisions.

How approval works

Inquiry

Describe the organization, domain, audience, intended placement, and proposed use.

Due diligence

We review claims, privacy practices, destination quality, category fit, and conflicts.

Go/No-Go Decision

Michelle coordinates the review. Each proposal receives a go/no-go decision based on relevance, evidence standards and current capacity. Some requests may be declined because they are not a fit at the current time.

Controlled setup

Approved widgets receive a domain-scoped token. Sponsorship creative receives a separate final review.

Measurement

Reporting is aggregate and limited to defined delivery and interaction events.

Non-negotiable boundaries

  • No access to calculator inputs or personalized results.
  • No sale of subscriber identities.
  • No personalized financial claims or guaranteed outcomes.
  • No pay-to-rank or methodology changes.
  • No arbitrary third-party scripts inside tools.
  • No placement goes live without final approval.
