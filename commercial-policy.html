Commercial Policy

Short version: payment does not determine WealthMeter calculations, rankings, methodologies, or editorial conclusions.

Owned products

WealthMeter publishes paid reports. A module labeled “WealthMeter report” links to a product published by WealthMeter. These placements are measured, but calculator inputs and result values are not transmitted as advertising data.

Affiliate links

If WealthMeter later uses an affiliate link, the relationship will be disclosed beside the recommendation. A purchase or application through such a link may produce a commission without changing the buyer's price. Inactive or unapproved partner offers are not displayed.

Rankings and sponsorships

Sponsored material will be labeled. A commercial partner cannot buy a score, rank, favorable finding, “best” designation, or methodology change. Any future comparison table will identify its criteria and will not confuse compensation with quality.

Financial standard

WealthMeter does not provide individualized investment, tax, insurance, or legal advice. Commercial material must not imply guaranteed returns, risk-free outcomes, or suitability for a specific reader.

Corrections and contact

Material conflicts, inaccurate disclosures, and commercial-policy concerns can be reported through the site's contact channel. See the Editorial Standards, Privacy Policy, and Disclaimer.
