What this calculator does — and does not do

Household measure

This page uses household net worth from the Census Bureau's Survey of Income and Program Participation. It is separate from WealthMeter's per-adult global model.

Published intervals

Results use the nine bands in Census Table 4. No distribution is assumed inside a band, so the output is a defensible percentile and rank interval.

Current release

The source is the 2025 SIPP wealth table for calendar year 2024, published July 15, 2026. The national median in Table 1 is $204,900.
