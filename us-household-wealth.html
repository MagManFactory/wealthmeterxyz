What this calculator does — and does not do

Household measure

This page uses household net worth from the Census Bureau's Survey of Income and Program Participation. It is separate from WealthMeter's per-adult global model.

Published Census core

Below $500,000, results use the published bands in Census Table 4 without assuming a distribution inside each band.

Hybrid upper tail

At $500,000 and above, the selected group's Census share is divided using the weighted national upper-tail shape in the Federal Reserve's 2022 SCF, converted to 2024 dollars.

Precision limit

The upper tail is a modeled range, not a published Census rank. Census households and SCF families are not identical units, uncertainty increases above $100 million, and the $1 billion-plus result remains open-ended.

Calculator sources

  • Census core and group shares2024 Wealth and Asset Ownership tables · official detailed workbook · SIPP methodology. Table 4 supplies the nine household wealth bands and the age, region, and housing-tenure shares.
  • Upper-tail shapeFederal Reserve 2022 Survey of Consumer Finances documentation · public summary extract. The weighted conditional distribution above $500,000 divides the Census table's open highest band.
  • Dollar-year adjustmentBLS historical CPI-U annual averages. SCF values are converted from 2022 to 2024 dollars using 313.689 ÷ 292.655 = 1.071873.
