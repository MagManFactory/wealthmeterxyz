How it works

Price is ranked only after quality gates pass.

  1. Convert the home spending budget using private-consumption price levels.
  2. Apply the selected quality floor to every available system measure.
  3. Require at least five of six measures, including life expectancy and essential services.
  4. Rank qualifying countries by a balance of quality preservation and estimated savings.

Data, limitations and interpretation

Price basis: World Bank private-consumption purchasing-power parity divided by the official exchange rate, indexed to the United States. This represents a national consumption basket, not a personal quotation.

Quality basis: World Development Indicators for life expectancy, electricity access, internet use, safely managed sanitation, trade and transport infrastructure, and intentional homicide. Years vary by country and measure and are shown in the result cards.

Not included: visas, taxes, housing tenure, healthcare eligibility, political conditions, language, climate, neighborhood variation, imported-goods exposure, or the user’s legal ability to relocate. Verify these separately before making a decision.
