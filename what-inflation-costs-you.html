Behind the numbers: sources, rights & limitations

Public-domain source, not a black box

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U), U.S. city average, not seasonally adjusted. Eight major expenditure groups plus all items. Annual averages, 2019–2024. Values are indexes, not dollar prices.

BLS CPI data · CPI FAQs · CPI methods

Reproducible calculation

For category i, Rᵢ(t) = indexᵢ(t) / indexᵢ(2019). Weight wᵢ = entered 2019 dollarsᵢ / total dollars. The basket index is 100 × Σ wᵢRᵢ(t); cumulative change is basket index − 100. Contribution in percentage points is 100 × wᵢ × [Rᵢ(t) − 1]. Headline CPI is rebased independently from its own series.

No external joins, interpolation, imputation or individual records. All inputs stay in this page; nothing is sent or saved. Reloading restores the default example.

What this can and cannot tell you

  • The presets are authored examples, not measured household types or official BLS expenditure weights.
  • This fixed-share blend is an educational price-exposure scenario. It does not recreate official CPI aggregation or substitution methods.
  • Housing includes owners’ equivalent rent, household energy and other housing items. It is not a measure of house prices, mortgage payments or a specific renter’s costs.
  • Broad categories conceal variation in products, locations and households. The published indexes are sample-based estimates; confidence intervals are not provided for this custom basket.
  • Annual averages are not December-to-December changes. The tool intentionally stops at 2024 for a common complete historical window; it is not a live 2026 inflation tracker. BLS reports that most 2025 annual averages omit October, so 2025 is excluded.
  • No income, taxes, asset returns, causal claims or predictions are included. Educational use only.
