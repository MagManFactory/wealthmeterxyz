Methodology (prototype)

Composite price-level index = weighted sum of category PLIs (housing, food, transport, health, recreation, education, general consumption). Weights shift by lifestyle mode; toggles zero out excluded categories and redistribute weight to general consumption.

  • Country layer: World Bank ICP household-consumption PPP patterns (illustrative, USA = 100).
  • City layer: Local rent/cost deltas on top of national PLI — prototype only.
  • Wealth Power Index: home composite PLI ÷ destination composite PLI.
  • Equivalent wealth: net worth × WPI multiplier.
  • Production path: swap purchasing-power-wealth-dev-data.js for live World Bank ICP API + OECD/Eurostat SDMX + IMF WEO inflation carry-forward.
