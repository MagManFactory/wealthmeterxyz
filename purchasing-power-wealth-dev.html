Methodology & data sources

Composite price-level index = weighted sum of category PLIs (housing, food, transport, healthcare, recreation, education, general consumption). Weights shift by lifestyle mode and household size.

  • Country layer: World Bank ICP household-consumption PPP patterns (illustrative, USA = 100).
  • City layer: Local rent/cost deltas on top of national PLI.
  • Wealth Power Index: home composite PLI ÷ destination composite PLI. Home = 1.0×.
  • Percentile bands: illustrative stubs until GWR tables are wired. Use the live global rank calculator for official wealth position.
  • Retirement model: 3.5% safe withdrawal + income; runway years are prototype estimates.

Production path: World Bank ICP API + OECD/Eurostat SDMX + IMF WEO inflation carry-forward + GWR percentile tables.
