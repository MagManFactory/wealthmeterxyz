Methodology & data sources (prototype)

Composite price-level index = weighted sum of category PLIs (housing, food, transport, healthcare, recreation, education, general consumption). Weights shift by lifestyle mode and household size; category toggles zero out excluded categories and redistribute weight to general consumption.

  • Country layer: World Bank ICP household-consumption PPP patterns (illustrative, USA = 100). 44 countries in this prototype.
  • City layer: Local rent/cost deltas on top of national PLI — 41 cities, prototype only.
  • Wealth Power Index (WPI): home composite PLI ÷ destination composite PLI. Home = 1.0×.
  • Equivalent wealth: nominal net worth × WPI multiplier.
  • Percentile stubs: illustrative bands — not live GWR data. Production will wire UBS GWR 2026 tables.
  • Retirement model: 3.5% safe withdrawal rate + annual income; runway = (assets + 10× income) ÷ required spend. Prototype only.
