Limits

Limits of this tool

  • BEA price parities end in 2024, the latest year BEA publishes.
  • Real per capita income is published for states only, not metros.
  • Parities compare price levels; they do not show local wages or taxes.
  • Personas are illustrative example inputs, not real people.

Sources and method

Where every number comes from

    All figures are read at page load from data/salary-worth.json, built by update_data.py from the BEA files below. The equivalent salary is your salary times the destination parity divided by the origin parity for the chosen year. Parities measure price levels for a typical consumption basket; your own basket (for example, if you own your home outright) may differ. Parities compare places within a year; to compare across years, BEA real income figures also remove national inflation. Pre tax comparison: state and local taxes are not included. Not financial advice.
