Limits

Limits of this tool

  • Rents and home values are Census ACS 2024 1-year medians. They are historical survey estimates, not current asking prices.
  • FHFA publishes some large metros only by division. Whole-metro growth then remains your assumption; select a named division for that division's published index.
  • 20 metros (Puerto Rico and Eagle Pass, TX) have no FHFA price index, so growth defaults are not available there.
  • Medians describe all homes and renters, not homes for sale today.
  • Income taxes and the mortgage interest deduction are not modeled.
  • Rent growth, investment return, tax, insurance, maintenance and transaction costs are your assumptions, not data.
  • Personas are illustrative example inputs, not real people.

Sources and method

Where every number comes from

    All data is read at page load from data/rent-vs-buy.json, built by update_data.py from the files below. ACS medians are survey estimates with margins of error (shown as plus or minus). ACS median gross rent includes utilities paid by the renter; median value is the owner's estimate of what the home would sell for. ACS covers all rented and owned units, not just homes for sale or vacant rentals, so current asking prices and rents may be higher. The FHFA all transactions index tracks repeat sales and appraisals of homes with conforming mortgages. FHFA publishes some large metros only as metro divisions. The page does not substitute one division for the whole metro; select a named division to use its own index. The calculator compounds monthly; loans are 30 year fixed. Not financial advice.
