Limits

Limits of this tool

  • Census top brackets are open ended ($100,000 and over for people, $200,000 and over for households), so above them results show as "Nth percentile or higher".
  • There is no state by age percentile. For states the page shows the median and wages instead.
  • Some BLS OEWS occupation wages are suppressed and show as n/a.
  • Income year is 2025; occupation wages are OEWS May 2025.
  • Income is pre-tax money income.
  • Personas are illustrative example inputs, not real people.

Sources and method

Where every number comes from

    All figures are read at page load from data/paycheck-rank.json, which update_data.py builds directly from the Census and BLS files. Percentiles are estimated by linear interpolation inside the published income brackets ($2,500 wide for people, $5,000 for households), so they can differ slightly from Census published medians, which are shown alongside. The top Census bracket is open ended ($100,000 and over for people, $200,000 and over for households), so above it the page can only say you are at or above a given percentile. Occupation ranks use the separate gross job-wage input, not Census total money income or household income. Occupation ranks interpolate between the BLS 10th, 25th, 50th, 75th and 90th percentile wages; beyond those points the page says below the 10th or above the 90th. Census income is total money income for calendar year 2025; BLS wages are May 2025 estimates. Not financial advice.

    WealthMeter special feature. Data: U.S. Census Bureau CPS ASEC and U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics OEWS.
