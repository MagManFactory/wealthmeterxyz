Limits

Limits of this tool

  • State detail covers only the 11 states the New York Fed publishes: AZ, CA, FL, IL, MI, NJ, NV, NY, OH, PA and TX.
  • Latest data is 2026 Q2. Q3 has not been released yet.
  • The New York Fed notes a temporary mortgage-reporting gap after a servicing transfer in 2026 Q2. Student-loan delinquency also reflects resumed reporting of defaulted balances. These changes are not solely borrower behavior.
  • Figures are national or state averages, not individual credit advice.
  • Personas are illustrative example inputs, not real people.

Sources and method

Where every number comes from

    All figures are read at page load from data/debt-map.json, which update_data.py builds directly from the New York Fed data workbook. Shares, changes and weighted averages on this page are simple arithmetic on those published values. The "your mix" comparison runs only in your browser and is not a credit score or a prediction.

    WealthMeter special feature. Data: Federal Reserve Bank of New York, Consumer Credit Panel/Equifax. Not financial advice. New York Fed Consumer Credit Panel / Equifax. WealthMeter calculations and charts are independent adaptations, not endorsed by the New York Fed. Source data use is subject to the New York Fed terms.
