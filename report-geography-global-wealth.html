The expensive misconception

Low cost does not always mean low barrier to entry.

A destination can look cheap online while requiring locked deposits, high income, international-school tuition, tax exposure, or a visa path that does not fit. The report calculates the full establishment problem rather than comparing restaurant prices.

Inside the field guide

Specific cities. Specific numbers. Explicit tradeoffs.

  • Ten destination profiles: Lisbon, Dubai, Medellín, Chiang Mai, Kuala Lumpur, Da Nang, Tulum and Playa del Carmen, Buenos Aires, Tbilisi, and Istanbul.
  • Seven-factor comparison: cost, tax, healthcare, residency, risk, family suitability, and remote-work infrastructure.

  • Standardized capital scenarios: including $500,000 allocations and realistic higher floors where required.
  • US citizen compliance chapter: plain-language coverage of FBAR, FATCA, FEIE, foreign tax credits, domicile, and exit-tax context.
  • Reconnaissance protocol and relocation worksheet: a practical sequence for testing a destination before moving.
