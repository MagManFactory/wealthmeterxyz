Installation pattern

The final token is issued only after approval. Publishers do not copy the demonstration URL.

<script src="https://wealthmeter.xyz/embed/wealth-rank.js" data-token="YOUR_SIGNED_DISTRIBUTION_TOKEN" async></script>
Domain control: the signed token, active distribution record, approved origin, campaign, widget identity, and validity dates must all agree. Moving the code to another domain disables the widget.

What is measured—and what is not

  • Qualified view after 50% visibility for one continuous second.
  • First interaction with the widget.
  • Approved publisher, campaign, widget, origin, and time.
  • No entered net worth, currency selection, rank, or percentile result.
  • No subscriber identity or host-page browsing profile.
  • No third-party sponsor script inside the calculator.
