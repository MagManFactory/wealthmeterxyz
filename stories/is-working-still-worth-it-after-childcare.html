Is working still worth it after childcare?

A $52,000 job and a $13,000 childcare bill leave a family with more to compare than the two headline amounts.

Tara, 34, is considering a return to work for $52,000 a year. She and Kevin have a four-year-old daughter. A childcare place would cost $250 a week, or $13,000 over 52 weeks. This is a hypothetical family; the salary, childcare price and year-round coverage are assumptions.

Subtracting the childcare bill from the salary leaves $39,000, but that is not Tara’s take-home contribution. Income and payroll taxes, commuting, work-related costs and any other care needs still have to be considered. A provider’s calendar may also differ from her work calendar.

The price is plausible as an illustration, not a local quote. In the Federal Reserve’s 2025 survey, $250 was the median weekly amount among parents who paid for childcare. The median was $350 among those using paid care for at least 20 hours a week. Neither figure establishes what a full-time place costs near Tara’s home.

The job could also include health coverage, retirement contributions and paid leave. Those terms need to be checked against the family’s current benefits, rather than counted as extras at their advertised value. A benefit Tara already has through Kevin may have a different value from one the household lacks.

There is a longer-term question too. Time out of paid work may affect experience, future earnings and retirement contributions. That does not make employment the right choice for every parent; it means a one-year subtraction cannot capture the whole decision.

Tara and Kevin can compare an actual offer with an actual care place, including hours, pickup arrangements, closures and travel. They can also make a backup plan for days their daughter cannot attend. If the schedule does not work, a positive annual total does not fix it.

The useful comparison is the change in the household’s finances and daily life under each option. A $13,000 bill can look overwhelming in isolation, just as a $52,000 salary can look decisive before costs. Both need the rest of the family’s circumstances beside them.
