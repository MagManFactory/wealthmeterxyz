Evidence and method

Why overlapping windows are not independent odds

Overlapping return windows share most of their years. The share of historical windows with a gain is a description of this record, not an independent probability of your future outcome.

By WealthMeter · Prepared with AI assistance · Sources and method reviewed 14 October 2026

Count the windows

The retained dataset has 98 annual observations, from 1928 through 2025. A complete 20-year holding period has 98 − 20 + 1 = 79 start years. Adjacent windows reuse 19 years of returns. Treating all 79 as independent trials would overstate the amount of distinct evidence.

What the chart can tell you

Inspect best, worst, median and loss windows as historical sensitivity to starting date. Longer horizons have fewer complete windows. A calm historical range can reflect the selected country, period and asset definitions as well as time. Use this page alongside the method and real-return explanations.

Limitations

The chart cannot prove that a holding period removes risk. Future regimes may differ, and this U.S. record does not represent every market. No confidence interval or calibrated future loss probability is supplied.
