Current Insight

Methodology

Medians for UBS GWR 2026 Table 1 markets are official UBS 2026 USD wealth-per-adult figures (report published 30 Jun 2026). Remaining Explorer markets, and all top-10% / top-1% thresholds, are unverified legacy presets. Some tails were copied from peer profiles. Population presets have not been reconciled to a dated UN table. This atlas is mixed-vintage: it is not a complete GWR 2026 54-country restatement.
