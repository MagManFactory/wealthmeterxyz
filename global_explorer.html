Understanding Data Grades
Grade A: Highly reliable. Utilizes comprehensive tax registries, administrative records, and national accounts.
Grade B: Reliable. Derived from high-quality household surveys with periodic administrative cross-checks.
Grade C: Modeled. Estimates based on limited survey data or econometric modeling from regional peers.
